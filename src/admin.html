    How to read these numbers

    Counts reflect checks admitted by the public rate limit. They are not a Twilio billing report: an attempt can be counted even when Lookup later fails.

    “Outcome untracked” is the gap between admitted checks and saved results, including checks made before result tracking began. A carrier name containing Twilio is a clue, not proof that Twilio currently hosts the number.
